## Returns & Refunds

### No-questions-asked window
<!-- topic: return_window -->

You can return any book within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund, no
reason needed. This is in line with UK Consumer Contracts Regulations.

To qualify:

- The book must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
- The book must be in **pristine condition, in its original packaging**.
- The return must include **proof of purchase** (order number or delivery note).

### Change-of-mind return costs
<!-- topic: change_of_mind_return_cost -->

For change-of-mind returns we refund the price of the book and the original
postage you paid, but **return postage is at the customer's own cost**. We
recommend using an economical tracked service. Items remain your property
until we receive them.

### Damaged items
<!-- topic: damaged_items -->

If your book arrived damaged, please contact us **within 72 hours of delivery**
with a photo of the damage. **Do not post damaged books back without
contacting us first.**

Depending on the damage, we may not need the book returned at all — in that
case we'll refund you and ask you to keep or recycle the book locally. Where
a return is needed, we cover the return postage via a prepaid label.

### Faulty items
<!-- topic: faulty_items -->

Faulty books (misprints, binding defects, missing pages) can be returned
within **7 working days** of discovering the fault. Some faults may only
become apparent later — contact us as soon as you notice.

We'll either replace the book or issue a full refund including original
postage. Return postage is covered by us.

### Wrong item sent
<!-- topic: wrong_item_sent -->

If we sent the wrong book by mistake, we'll send a prepaid return label and
either refund in full or ship the correct item at no cost to you.

### Refund timing
<!-- topic: refund_timing -->

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** within **3-5 business days**
of approval. Your bank may take an additional 1-2 days to show the credit.

### Items that cannot be returned
<!-- topic: non_returnable_items -->

The following cannot be returned:

- Gift cards and eGifts (once electronically delivered).
- Sealed audio, video, DVD, or computer software once the seal is broken.
- Books customised with personal inscriptions or special orders.

## Delivery

### UK delivery cost
<!-- topic: uk_delivery_cost -->

Standard UK delivery is **£2.99** on orders under £20, and **free on orders
of £20 or more**.

### UK delivery times
<!-- topic: uk_delivery_time -->

UK orders typically arrive in **2-4 working days from dispatch**. Orders
placed before **2pm GMT** are dispatched the same working day, subject to
stock availability. You'll receive a dispatch confirmation email with a
tracking number once your order leaves our warehouse.

### UK carriers
<!-- topic: uk_carriers -->

Most UK orders are sent via **Royal Mail Tracked 48** or **Evri**. Larger or
higher-value orders may ship via **DPD** or **Parcelforce**. We do not
currently offer a standard next-day option.

### Order tracking
<!-- topic: order_tracking -->

If you ordered through a Bookly account, you can log in at any time to check
order progress. If you checked out as a guest, the dispatch confirmation
email contains your tracking link.

### Order cancellation
<!-- topic: order_cancellation -->

Orders can be cancelled **within 60 minutes of placing**, or any time before
they enter **"Processing"** status. Once an order has been dispatched, it
cannot be cancelled — please use the returns process after delivery.

## Account

### Password reset
<!-- topic: password_reset -->

If you've forgotten your password, click **"Forgot password?"** on the sign-in
page. We'll email a secure reset link to the address on your account — the
link expires after **60 minutes** and can only be used once.

If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes:

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Confirm you used the same email address you signed up with.
- Add `no-reply@bookly.com` to your contacts so future mails don't get filtered.

For security we never share passwords over chat, email, or phone. If you no
longer have access to the email on the account, contact support with your
order number so we can verify ownership.

## Contact

For returns, damaged items, or delivery questions, contact our support team
and include your **order number** so we can help you quickly.
